METHOD — how this list was built
  • Source: every coin that graduated (bonded) off pump.tires — the full launchpad walk, ~1,000 graduates out of ~37,000 launches, re-scanned daily.
  • Paid filter: the coin has a paid DexScreener profile, proven two ways — DexScreener's own order record (/orders/v1, approval date in the PAID column) AND a logo served by DexScreener's CDN. A coin failing either is not in the set and is not shown anywhere.
  • Mcap: FDV = total supply × price, read on-chain from the coin's deepest PulseX pool (V1 or V2) and cross-checked against pump.tires. Cutoff sub-55.55M PLS MCap (≈ $577 today) — fixed in PLS so it never drifts with the PLS price. A FULL LIST coin priced over the cutoff is hidden until it comes back under it (48 hidden right now).
  • SAVED: a coin that gets revived — FULL LIST or TANGENT'S PICKS → SAVED when its FDV reaches 555,500,000 PLS (≈ $5,765 today); back to FULL LIST only under the cutoff. Nothing moves in between.
  • LP filter: at most 1 real token-token pool (PulseX V1 + V2, from the subgraph). A pool counts only if it holds ≥ 5% of the coin's own cap — dust pools are ignored.
  • FELL OFF is the error bucket only: more than one real token-token pool, or a manual move. Price never puts a coin here.
  • TANGENT'S PICKS and manual SAVED rows are hand-picked and locked; the rules refresh their numbers but never move them.
  • Vibes filter: subjective "good meme" pass — manual, not scored.
  • Auto-refresh: daily at 03:15 UTC — pump.tires walk, on-chain caps, paid + logo check, sections, republish. The time above the table is the last refresh.
  • Source of truth: master.json — every coin ever seen; coins only move between sections (or are excluded), never deleted.
